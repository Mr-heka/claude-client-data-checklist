# Client data checklist

Claude asks which plan you are on, then walks you through the privacy setting, what deleting a chat does, incognito chats and the Team plan default. Every point comes from Anthropic's own pages.

## Use it

Open `SETUP-PROMPT.md`, copy the text in the box into a new chat in Claude, Claude Code or Codex, and follow along.

## The checklist, in short

- **1. Settings, then Privacy.** On Free, Pro and Max, find Help improve our AI models. On, your chats can be used to improve Claude. Off, your new chats are not used for future training.
- **2. Off does not mean nothing is kept.** Your chats stay in your history until you delete them. A deleted chat leaves your history straight away and Anthropic's back-end storage within 30 days.
- **3. Incognito for one-off client jobs.** New chat outside a project, then the ghost icon, top right. Not used for training, not saved to your history or memory, kept 30 days by default.
- **4. Team and Enterprise.** By default, Anthropic does not use inputs or outputs from its commercial products to train its models.
