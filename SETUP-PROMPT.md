# Client data checklist: copy this into Claude

Copy everything in the box below into a new chat in Claude, Claude Code or Codex.

```text
https://github.com/Mr-heka/claude-client-data-checklist

Walk me through the Selr AI client data checklist for Claude, one step at a time. Every point below comes from Anthropic's own privacy and help pages (privacy.claude.com and support.claude.com). The repository above has the same list with each source link.

First ask me which Claude plan I am on: Free, Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise. Then take me through these in order and wait for me after each one.

1. Free, Pro or Max: open Settings, then Privacy, and find "Help improve our AI models". On means my chats and coding sessions can be used to improve Claude. Off means my new chats and coding sessions will not be used for future model training. Turning it off does not remove anything from training that has already started. Help me decide which I want.

2. Off does not mean nothing is kept. My chats stay in my chat history until I delete them. When I delete a chat it is removed from my history straight away and deleted from Anthropic's back-end storage within 30 days.

3. For a one-off client job, start a new chat outside a project and click the ghost icon in the top right for an incognito chat. Incognito chats are not used for training and are not saved to my chat history or memory. They are kept for 30 days by default.

4. Team or Enterprise: by default Anthropic does not use inputs or outputs from its commercial products (Claude for Work, the API and others) to train its models.

Keep it in plain English. This is a checklist, not legal advice. If I ask about my obligations under privacy law, say that and point me to https://privacy.claude.com and my own adviser.
```
